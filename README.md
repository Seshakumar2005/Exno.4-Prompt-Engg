# Exno.3-Scenario-Based Report Development Utilizing Diverse Prompting Techniques
### DATE: 30-04-2025                                                                           
### REGISTER NUMBER : 212222060235
### Aim: To design an AI-powered chatbot that assists customers in resolving issues related to product troubleshooting, order tracking, and general inquiries. The chatbot should handle various customer queries efficiently while maintaining a conversational and user-friendly tone. In this experiment, we will employ different prompt patterns to guide the development process of the chatbot, ranging from basic task-oriented prompts to more complex, persona-driven prompts.

Scenario:    
Current Research on AI and Healthcare Integration (2025)    
    
Aim    
•	To explore how diverse prompting techniques can enhance scenario-based report development.    
•	To apply and evaluate different prompting methods (Straightforward, Preceding, and Tabular) using current AI healthcare research as the scenario.    
•	To understand how each technique influences the clarity, depth, and structure of information generation.    
    
1.	Straightforward Prompting    
Definition:    
Straightforward prompting involves giving clear and direct instructions to the AI without requiring detailed step-by-step reasoning. This technique is used when concise and immediate responses are needed.    
Application to Scenario:    
Using the straightforward prompting method, the task was to summarize the major findings of recent (2025) research on AI integration in healthcare. The AI generated a focused and informative overview:    
•	AI technologies have significantly improved diagnostic accuracy, with studies reporting a 30–40% increase compared to traditional methods.    
•	Robotic surgeries are now achieving higher precision, leading to reduced complication rates and faster patient recovery times.    
•	Predictive analytics have advanced early detection of chronic diseases, improving patient outcomes.    
•	Administrative AI systems are contributing to a 25% reduction in hospital operational costs by streamlining workflows and minimizing human errors.    
By applying straightforward prompting, the report quickly captured the essential trends without unnecessary complexity, making it highly suitable for executive summaries and briefings.    
    
    
2.	Preceding Prompting    
Definition:    
Preceding prompting builds a chain of prompts where each new instruction depends on or deepens the previous output. This technique fosters logical progression and a layered understanding of complex topics.    
Application to Scenario:    
Initially, the AI was asked to list the primary benefits of AI in healthcare based on current research. The response highlighted faster diagnoses, more personalized treatments, and improved operational efficiency. Building on that, a subsequent prompt requested an explanation of how AI improves operational efficiency. The AI detailed that administrative tasks, such as patient scheduling, resource management, and billing, are now largely automated by AI systems, leading to significant time savings and reduction in manual errors. Finally, a further prompt asked for a proposed improvement to hospital management systems using AI. The suggestion was the development of real-time AI-based monitoring systems that could optimize hospital operations dynamically, predict bottlenecks before they occur, and automatically adjust resource allocation.    
Through preceding prompting, the report moved from general benefits to specific mechanisms and future enhancements, demonstrating how layered prompting can construct a coherent, evolving analysis.    
    
3. Tabular Prompting    
Definition:   
Tabular prompting is a method of organizing complex information into a structured table format, allowing for clear visualization, easy comparison, and quick reference.   
Application to Scenario:   
The task involved generating a table that compares the advantages and disadvantages of AI integration in healthcare, based on the most recent research findings from 2025.   
   
     
   
   
  
  
Table Output:    
   
ASPECT  	ADVANTAGE  	DISADVANTAGE  
DIAGNOSTICS  	Improved accuracy and speed  	Risk of AI misdiagnosis and over-reliance  
TREATMENT  	More personalized and targeted therapies  	Potential biases within AI algorithms  
MANAGEMENT  	Cost reduction and operational efficiency  	Data privacy and security vulnerabilities  
   
  
  
  
  
Using tabular prompting allowed the report to concisely present multiple perspectives sideby-side, enhancing clarity for the reader.    
    
Conclusion    
This experiment clearly demonstrates the effectiveness of using multiple prompting techniques for scenario-based report development.    
•	Straightforward prompting is ideal for generating clear, concise summaries of research findings.    
•	Preceding prompting enables a natural, logical build-up of information, enriching the depth and connectivity between ideas.    
•	Tabular prompting organizes complex information into accessible and comparative formats.    
Applying these techniques to the current research on AI in healthcare (2025) resulted in a well-structured, detailed, and reader-friendly report. Choosing the right prompting technique based on the report’s goal can significantly enhance the quality and usability of the final output.    
     
RESULT:    
The application of diverse prompting techniques improved the structure, clarity, and depth of the scenario-based report.    
Straightforward, preceding, and tabular prompting each contributed uniquely to organizing and expanding information.    
This method proved effective in presenting complex research on AI in healthcare (2025) clearly and logically.
